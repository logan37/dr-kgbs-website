No paid SEO data source (traffic, backlinks, domain authority) was used. Competitor sites were not crawled, so their schema and technical setup are not assessed here; run `/seo page <url>` on any of them for that.

## Gaps the competition leaves open

1. **Test-specific local pages.** Few results explain a PFT or allergy skin test in detail for a Kondapur patient: preparation, duration, pain, cost, same-day results.
2. **The doctor performs the test.** Hospitals rarely say who runs the spirometer. This is a real, checkable difference.
3. **Allergy plus lung care in one place.** Most pulmonology pages treat allergy as a side item. The CMC Vellore allergy diploma is a strong, specific credential.
4. **Telugu content.** Results reviewed were in English only. Telugu versions of the top test and condition pages are an option for Phase 3.
5. **Sleep apnea.** Sleep study searches are served mostly by hospitals; a clear page on home sleep testing could rank.

## What to copy

- Hospital sites list doctors with qualifications and photos on the first screen. Do the same.
- Directories show consultation fees. Showing a fee or range reduces calls asking only about cost.
- KIMS and Sunshine name their equipment (pulmonary function lab). Name the clinic's spirometer and sleep study setup.
